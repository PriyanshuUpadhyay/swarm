# Apple UI lens

These rules check UI states, forms, motion, layout, and accessibility in SwiftUI, UIKit, and AppKit
views. The Apple platforms lens (`AP-`) owns icon-only labels, tap gestures in place of `Button`,
fixed-size fonts, unlocalized strings, the 44 pt hit target, and color-only state; these rules do not
repeat them.

Files: `**/*.swift`

- `UA-1` A data view (list, detail screen, search result) that shows only the loaded content, with no loading, empty, or error state → a placeholder or `ProgressView` while loading, `ContentUnavailableView` (or equal) for empty and for error, and a retry on error. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/loading (show that content is loading and what to do when there is none) ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/contentunavailableview
- `UA-2` A button that starts async work (save, send, sign in, purchase) and stays enabled while it runs, or an action with no in-flight guard → `.disabled(isWorking)` (or `isEnabled = false`) from start to finish, a guard at the top of the action, and a `ProgressView` in or next to the button. Applies: `Button|UIButton|UIAction|addTarget|onSubmit|\.task|Task\s*\{|async|await`. Source: Impeccable harden guide (Concurrent operations: prevent double submission by disabling while loading)
- `UA-3` A destructive action (delete, remove, discard, sign out, leave) that runs at once, or a destructive button without `role: .destructive` → `.confirmationDialog` or an alert with a `.destructive` and a `.cancel` button, or an undo path. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/alerts (confirm a destructive action and give a way to cancel) ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/confirmationdialog(_:ispresented:titlevisibility:actions:message:)
- `UA-4` An optimistic model change made before the server confirms, with no rollback when the request fails → keep the old value, restore it in the `catch`, and show the error. Source: Impeccable harden guide (Concurrent operations: optimistic updates with rollback; otherwise the screen shows a change that was never saved)
- `UA-5` A form error shown only in an alert after submit, not next to its field, or a failed submit that clears the fields → an inline message under the field, keep the entered text, and move `@FocusState` to the first invalid field. Applies: `TextField|SecureField|TextEditor|UITextField|NSTextField|Form|@FocusState|onSubmit|validat`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/entering-data (validate fields and show where the problem is; do not make people enter data again)
- `UA-6` A new animation (`withAnimation`, `.animation`, `.transition`, `matchedGeometryEffect`, a spring, `UIView.animate`, `repeatForever`, auto-scroll, parallax) that does not check Reduce Motion → read `@Environment(\.accessibilityReduceMotion)` (`UIAccessibility.isReduceMotionEnabled`) and use a cross-fade or no motion. Applies: `withAnimation|\.animation\(|\.transition\(|matchedGeometryEffect|spring|UIView\.animate|UIViewPropertyAnimator|CABasicAnimation|CAKeyframeAnimation|phaseAnimator|keyframeAnimator|symbolEffect|repeatForever|scrollTo\(|contentTransition`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion (respect the Reduce Motion setting) ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/environmentvalues/accessibilityreducemotion
- `UA-7` Content that jumps when data or images arrive: `AsyncImage` with no fixed frame or aspect ratio, a spinner replaced by content of another size, rows inserted above the visible one → reserve the final size (`.frame`, `.aspectRatio`, `.redacted(reason: .placeholder)` on sample content) and keep the scroll position. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/loading (show placeholder content in the final layout while loading)
- `UA-8` Layout that breaks at large Dynamic Type sizes: a fixed `.frame(height:)` or `lineLimit(1)` around text, hardcoded spacing or icon sizes beside text, an `HStack` of text and controls that cannot wrap → `@ScaledMetric` for sizes, no fixed height on text, and a vertical layout at accessibility sizes (`dynamicTypeSize.isAccessibilitySize` or `ViewThatFits`). Applies: `\.frame\(|lineLimit|HStack|fixedSize|@ScaledMetric|dynamicTypeSize|heightAnchor|constant:`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography (support Dynamic Type and check the layout at the largest sizes) ; SwiftUI Expert Skill (accessibility patterns: `@ScaledMetric`)
- `UA-9` A cap on text scaling: `.dynamicTypeSize(...)` with an upper bound, `minimumScaleFactor` on body text, or `maximumContentSizeCategory` on a whole screen → remove the cap; limit it only on a chrome element that cannot grow, and say why. Applies: `dynamicTypeSize\(|minimumScaleFactor|maximumContentSizeCategory|minimumContentSizeCategory|adjustsFontSizeToFitWidth`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility (people choose the text size they can read)
- `UA-10` A custom control or composite row with missing VoiceOver semantics: a toggle-like or selectable view with no `.isSelected`/`.isToggle` trait or `accessibilityValue`, a section title with no `.isHeader`, an image-plus-text row read as separate stops, a custom slider or stepper with no `accessibilityAdjustableAction` → add the trait and value, group with `.accessibilityElement(children: .combine)`, or use `.accessibilityRepresentation` with a system control. Source: SwiftUI Expert Skill (accessibility patterns: traits, grouping, custom controls) ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/accessibilityaddtraits(_:)
- `UA-11` A state change that VoiceOver users cannot see (loading finished, error shown, item saved, count changed) with no announcement or focus move → `AccessibilityNotification.Announcement(...).post()` (`UIAccessibility.post`) or move accessibility focus with `@AccessibilityFocusState`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/accessibility/accessibilitynotification/announcement (post an announcement for a change that is not otherwise conveyed)
- `UA-12` An action reachable only by swipe, long press, drag, or context menu, with no VoiceOver or keyboard path → `.accessibilityAction(named:)` for the same action, or a visible button or menu item. Applies: `swipeActions|onLongPressGesture|DragGesture|MagnifyGesture|RotateGesture|\.gesture\(|draggable|onDrag|onDrop|contextMenu|UIPanGestureRecognizer|UISwipeGestureRecognizer|UILongPressGestureRecognizer|UIContextMenuInteraction`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/gestures (give other ways to do an action a gesture does) ; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/accessibilityaction(named:_:)
- `UA-13` A hardcoded color for text, background, or separator (`Color(red:green:blue:)`, a hex initializer, `.white`, `.black`, `UIColor(red:...)`, `NSColor(calibratedRed:...)`, a color literal) → a semantic color (`.primary`, `.secondary`, `Color(.systemBackground)`, `.separator`) or an asset catalog color with a dark variant. Applies: `Color\(red:|Color\(hex|Color\(white:|Color\(#|\.(white|black)\b|UIColor\(red:|UIColor\(white:|UIColor\(hex|NSColor\(red:|NSColor\(calibrated|NSColor\(white:|colorLiteral`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dark-mode (use system or asset colors that adapt; fixed colors break in the other mode)
- `UA-14` A screen or app forced to one appearance (`.preferredColorScheme(.light)`, `overrideUserInterfaceStyle = .light`, `UIUserInterfaceStyle` in Info.plist) with no stated product reason → support both light and dark. Files: `**/*.swift, **/*.plist`. Applies: `preferredColorScheme|overrideUserInterfaceStyle|UIUserInterfaceStyle|\.colorScheme\(`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dark-mode (people expect apps to follow their appearance setting)
- `UA-15` Text over a material, blur, or translucent layer (or a new translucent surface) with no fallback for Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast → read `accessibilityReduceTransparency` and `colorSchemeContrast` and switch to an opaque surface or stronger contrast. Applies: `Material|ultraThin|thinMaterial|regularMaterial|thickMaterial|\.blur\(|UIBlurEffect|UIVisualEffectView|NSVisualEffectView|glassEffect|\.opacity\(`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/environmentvalues/accessibilityreducetransparency ; Apple Design guide (reduced transparency makes surfaces solid)
- `UA-16` Text whose contrast is too low: gray on gray, text or icons dimmed with `.opacity(...)`, a custom secondary color → at least 4.5:1 for body text (3:1 for large text), and `.secondary`/`.tertiary` styles over opacity. Applies: `foregroundStyle|foregroundColor|textColor|\.opacity\(|\.gray|Color\(|tintColor`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color (ensure sufficient contrast) ; https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility
- `UA-17` A custom look-alike of a system control or container (a toggle drawn from shapes, a custom segmented control, picker, alert overlay, navigation bar, tab bar, or back button) → the system control (`Toggle`, `Picker`, `.alert`, `NavigationStack` toolbar, `TabView`) styled with its style protocol. Source: SwiftUI Expert Skill (prefer native SwiftUI APIs; follow the Human Interface Guidelines) (system controls bring VoiceOver, keyboard, Dynamic Type, and dark mode support)
- `UA-18` The system back button or navigation bar hidden and replaced (`navigationBarBackButtonHidden(true)`, `hidesBackButton`, a disabled `interactivePopGestureRecognizer`) → keep the system back button and swipe-back, and add items with `.toolbar`. Applies: `navigationBarBackButtonHidden|hidesBackButton|interactivePopGestureRecognizer|toolbar\(\.hidden|navigationBarHidden|setNavigationBarHidden`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/toolbars (the back button and swipe-back are expected ways to return)
- `UA-19` A sheet or full-screen cover with no dismiss path (no Cancel or Done, `interactiveDismissDisabled()` with no other exit), or an edit sheet that drops unsaved changes on swipe-down → a Cancel/Done in the toolbar, and `interactiveDismissDisabled(hasChanges)` with a discard confirmation. Applies: `sheet|fullScreenCover|interactiveDismissDisabled|isModalInPresentation|present\(|modalPresentationStyle|popover`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets (a sheet needs a clear way to close; confirm before discarding changes)
- `UA-20` Text or buttons that cannot hold longer or translated strings: a fixed `.frame(width:)` on text or a button, `lineLimit(1)` on translatable text with no plan for truncation, a label width constraint → let it wrap or grow, and truncate only where the full text is available elsewhere. Applies: `\.frame\(|lineLimit|widthAnchor|truncationMode|numberOfLines|constant:`. Source: Impeccable harden guide (translations run 30 to 40 percent longer; avoid fixed widths on text) ; https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing
- `UA-21` Left/right in place of leading/trailing (`.left`, `.right`, `leftAnchor`, `NSTextAlignment.left`, `contentHorizontalAlignment = .left`), or a directional image (arrow, chevron) that does not flip in right-to-left → leading/trailing APIs, `.natural` alignment, and `.flipsForRightToLeftLayoutDirection(true)` on directional images. Applies: `\.left\b|\.right\b|leftAnchor|rightAnchor|textAlignment|contentHorizontalAlignment|arrow|chevron|flipsForRightToLeft|layoutDirection`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/right-to-left (layouts and directional images mirror in right-to-left languages)
- `UA-22` A date, number, currency, measurement, or list built with a hardcoded format (`dateFormat = "MM/dd/yyyy"`, `String(format: "%.2f")`, `joined(separator: ", ")`), or a plural built by string concatenation → `Text(date, format:)`, `.formatted()`, `FormatStyle`, `Array.formatted()`, and String Catalog plural variants. Source: SwiftUI Expert Skill (localization: format styles adapt to the locale; never glue localized fragments)
- `UA-23` Content that ignores the safe area: `ignoresSafeArea()` on readable or interactive content, hardcoded top or bottom padding standing in for the notch or home indicator, a bar laid in a `ZStack` or overlay that covers scrolling content → scope `ignoresSafeArea` to background layers and named edges, and place bars with `safeAreaInset(edge:)` or `safeAreaBar(edge:)`. Applies: `ignoresSafeArea|edgesIgnoringSafeArea|safeArea|\.padding\(|\.overlay|ZStack|statusBarFrame|additionalSafeAreaInsets|contentInsetAdjustmentBehavior|safeAreaLayoutGuide`. Source: SwiftUI Expert Skill (layout best practices: safe areas) ; https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout (keep content inside the safe area)
- `UA-24` Layout sized from global screen state (`UIScreen.main.bounds`, `NSScreen.main`, a fixed device width, `userInterfaceIdiom` in place of size class) → size from the container (`containerRelativeFrame`, `GeometryReader`, `horizontalSizeClass`). Applies: `UIScreen\.main|NSScreen\.main|\.bounds\.(width|height)|userInterfaceIdiom|UIDevice\.current`. Source: SwiftUI Expert Skill (read layout inputs nearest the view; do not use process-global screen state) (split view, Stage Manager, and rotation give a window smaller than the screen)
- `UA-25` A text field without the input setup its content needs: no `textContentType` on name, email, password, one-time code, or address; the wrong `keyboardType`; autocorrection or capitalization left on for emails, codes, and usernames; no `submitLabel`/`onSubmit` to go to the next field → set them. Applies: `TextField|SecureField|UITextField|TextEditor|NSTextField|textContentType|keyboardType`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields (match the keyboard and content type to the data, so AutoFill and the right keys appear)
- `UA-26` A focus indicator removed (`.focusEffectDisabled()`, `focusRingType = .none`) with no replacement, or a custom interactive view that full keyboard access cannot reach → keep the system focus effect or draw one, and use `Button` or `.focusable(interactions: .activate)`. Applies: `focusEffectDisabled|focusRingType|focusable|@FocusState|onKeyPress`. Source: SwiftUI Expert Skill (focus patterns) ; https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/keyboards (people navigate and act with the keyboard)
- `UA-27` A row or card button whose tap area is only its text or icon (a `.plain` button with a `Spacer`, a label padded outside the button, a row with a trailing chevron) → put the padding inside the label and add `.contentShape(Rectangle())` so the whole visible row responds. Applies: `Button|buttonStyle|\.plain|Spacer\(|contentShape|NavigationLink`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/contentshape(_:eofill:) (the hit area follows the drawn content unless a shape is set)
- `UA-28` A user-facing error that shows only a raw `error.localizedDescription`, an error code, or "Something went wrong" → say what happened and what to do next, with a retry or other action button. Applies: `(?i)error|fail|alert|localizedDescription|catch`. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/alerts (an alert message explains the problem and the way forward) ; https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing
